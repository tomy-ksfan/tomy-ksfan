## About me

I'm a Statistics Ph.D. student and graduate researcher at **The Ohio State University**. I develop statistical and machine-learning methods for complex, noisy, and biased data, with current research focused on matrix completion under non-uniform missingness and Bayesian state-space models for digital twin systems.

I hold an **M.S. in Statistics** from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and a **B.S. in Applied Mathematics** from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University. My background also includes financial-risk modeling, large-scale text analysis, and large language model evaluation.

I'm interested in research collaborations and internship opportunities in applied machine learning, quantitative research, and statistical modeling.

## Research interests

- **Matrix completion:** inverse-probability-weighted estimation, propensity-score correction, and simulation studies
- **Dynamic systems:** Bayesian state-space modeling, real-time estimation, uncertainty quantification, and robustness
- **Applied machine learning:** predictive modeling, model validation, financial risk, and large-scale data analysis

## Experience highlights
